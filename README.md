# Next Shore · 彼岸

**中文** — 一个给工作 1–5 年、英语一般、对出国有好奇但没有概念的人的自我评估原型。先用 7 步评估看清“我现在在哪”，雷达图实时变化；结果给出路线图、时间线、投入程度，以及按你最弱项解释的“对我来说要做到什么”。国家作为第二层参考，只列技能打分、雇主担保、留学转工作三类普通人路线，不含任何投资/购房移民。

**English** — A self-assessment prototype for people 1–5 years into their careers, with average English and curiosity about living abroad. A 7-step assessment with a live radar shows where you stand; results give a roadmap, timeline, effort level and a "What would it take for me?" explanation based on your weakest axes. Countries are a secondary layer covering only skilled, employer-sponsored and study-to-work routes — no investment or property schemes.

- `index.html` — UI, styles, vanilla JS (no build step) / 界面与交互
- `data.js` — questions, scoring functions, archetypes, 22 countries (5 fully populated) / 题目、评分、结果类型、国家数据

**Disclaimer / 说明**: general information only, not advice. Figures are approximate; check official sources. 仅为一般信息，不构成建议，数字均为近似，请以官方为准。
